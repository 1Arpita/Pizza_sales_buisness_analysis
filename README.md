🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis using MySQL & Excel

A complete end-to-end data analysis project focused on analyzing pizza sales data using **MySQL** and **Microsoft Excel** to uncover business insights related to revenue, customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends.

---

📌 Project Overview

This project was developed to analyze transactional pizza sales data and transform raw business data into actionable insights through SQL analysis and interactive dashboard visualization.

The project covers:

- Database creation  
- Data cleaning  
- SQL-based business analysis  
- KPI reporting  
- Trend analysis  
- Interactive Excel dashboard creation  
- Business recommendations  

---

🎯 Business Problem Statement

Pizza businesses generate large amounts of transactional data every day. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify:

- Peak sales periods  
- Customer ordering behavior  
- Best-selling products  
- Revenue-driving categories  
- Operational trends  

The goal of this project is to analyze pizza sales data and build a dashboard that supports data-driven business decision-making.

---

🚀 Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and sales performance  
- Identify peak ordering days and hours  
- Understand customer preferences by pizza category and size  
- Identify best-selling and low-performing pizzas  
- Build an interactive dashboard for reporting  
- Generate business recommendations from sales insights

 🔍 Key Business Insights

## 📈 Peak Sales Timing

- Highest orders occur on Friday and Saturday  
- Peak ordering hours are between 12 PM–1 PM and 5 PM–8 PM  

---

 🍕 Category Performance

- Classic pizzas contribute the highest share of sales and orders  

---

📏 Pizza Size Analysis

- Large pizzas generate the highest revenue contribution  

---

🏆 Best-Selling Products

Top-performing pizzas include:

- Pepperoni Pizza  
- Barbecue Chicken Pizza  
- California Chicken Pizza  

---

📉 Lowest-Performing Products

Low-performing pizzas include:

- Brie Carre Pizza
